Rethinking On-Policy Distillation of Large Language Models: Phenomenology, Mechanism, and Recipe

Yaxuan Li, Yuxin Zuo, Bingxiang He, Jinqian Zhang, Chaojun Xiao, Cheng Qian, Tianyu Yu, Huan-ang Gao, Wenkai Yang, Zhiyuan Liu, Ning Ding

This work studies on-policy distillation for large language models, analyzing its phenomenology and mechanism, and proposing a practical recipe for effective distillation.
